**v2.0.0 — The upgraded MetaStation platform.** Here is everything in this release.

{/* truncate */}

## Social Trade

- **Browse strategies, fund managers and signal providers in one list**, ranked by a published
  0–100 score — never by raw return. Grades, labels (including the cautionary ones), filters,
  compare, watch, lists and a **Fresh performers** rail. → [Browse Providers](/docs/social-trading/browse-marketplace) · [How Providers Are Scored](/docs/social-trading/scoring)
- **Readable without an account.** Discover, providers, profiles, indexes and the methodology open
  signed out; copying asks you to sign in and brings you back.
- **MetaStation Indexes** — copy a set of scored strategies into your own account by fixed, published
  rules. → [Indexes](/docs/social-trading/indexes)
- **Follower risk limits.** A loss limit and a trailing stop that stop copying for you, a daily loss
  limit, market and direction filters, caps on positions and margin, your own take-profit and
  stop-loss, and *copy only trades with a stop-loss*. → [Copy Settings](/docs/social-trading/copy-settings)
- **Countertrade** — take the opposite side of a provider's trades.
- **Your leverage, never the provider's.** Copies open at your account's leverage for the market, or
  one you set.
- **Prepaid subscriptions** in the Store, with your price locked in for as long as you stay
  subscribed. Fund managers are paid a share of profit only when a copy exits in profit.
- **Provider tiers, invite-only products and a provider hub.** → [Become a Provider](/docs/social-trading/become-provider)
- **Public profiles at metastation.fi/@handle.** → [Your Public Profile](/docs/social-trading/trader-profile)

MetaStation does not manage your funds: every copy executes in your own account.

## Accounts and Telegram

- **Continue with Telegram** — sign in with your Telegram account or your Telegram wallet, and use
  MetaStation inside Telegram through [@MetaStationBot](https://t.me/MetaStationBot). → [MetaStation in Telegram](/docs/getting-started/telegram)
- **Every MetaStation Account is free**, with every automation tool. Its first deposit pays a
  one-time activation fee, shown before you send.

## Wallet and security

- **Deposits name the wallet first** — the Funding Wallet (USDC on Arbitrum One, or bridged from an
  EVM network, Solana or TON) or, after activation, the Trading Account for BTC, ETH, SOL and more.
  Delayed bridged deposits can be refunded.
- **One confirmation code per withdrawal** — from your authenticator, or by e-mail up to $1,000 a
  day. Sanctions screening on every destination. Multi-step withdrawals show each step.
- **Sign out everywhere**, and an opt-in **dead man's switch**. → [Dead Man's Switch](/docs/security/dead-mans-switch)

## Trading

- **Cancel All** on every venue cancels the pair's take-profit and stop-loss orders too.
- Leverage up to each market's own maximum; fee rates on the exchange tickets; funding payments and
  predicted funding; KuCoin spot margin.

## Automation

- **Signed webhooks** and a **TradingView source-address check**, so a leaked URL is useless.
  → [Secure Your Webhook](/docs/guides/secure-your-webhook)
- **`target`** signals — one message to hold long, short or flat — and **dry runs** that place
  nothing. → [Supported Actions](/docs/developer/supported-actions)
- Repeated requests within 60 seconds run once; stale opening signals are refused.
- Telegram channels are found from your own Telegram session, can be paused, and limit who can
  trigger a trade.

## Documentation

- Every page rewritten against the upgraded platform, with new pages for scoring, indexes, public
  profiles, Telegram and the dead man's switch.
