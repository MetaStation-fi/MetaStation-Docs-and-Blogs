# Guides

**Each of these is one job, start to finish, ending in something that works.**

Where the reference sections describe what a feature does, these describe how to get a specific
outcome — including the risk settings to configure first and the failure modes to expect.

---

## Automation

**[Send TradingView Alerts to Binance Automatically](/docs/guides/tradingview-webhook-to-binance)**
The full path from a TradingView alert to a live Binance position: connecting the exchange,
unlocking automation, the alert payload, and the placeholders that let one alert serve every
signal. *~15 minutes.*

**[Auto-Execute Telegram Signals](/docs/guides/auto-execute-telegram-signals)**
Turn a signal channel into trades — connect Telegram, let the channel be detected, map it to an
account, and cap what an unknown caller can do with your balance. *~10 minutes.*

**[Trade from Your Own Script or Bot](/docs/guides/automate-with-a-script)**
One HTTP POST, with working Python and Node, error handling that keeps local state honest, and the
mistakes that cost money. *For developers.*

**[Secure Your Webhook URL](/docs/guides/secure-your-webhook)**
The URL is a bearer credential. Where they leak from, how to restrict one in advance, and what to
do in the first five minutes after one is exposed. **Read this before the others go live.**

---

## Trading

**[Scale Out with Multiple Take Profits](/docs/guides/scale-out-with-take-profits)**
Build a take-profit ladder that banks part of a move while the rest runs — where to place levels,
how to split size, and an honest account of what a ladder does and does not improve.

**[Make a Trade Risk-Free with SLX](/docs/guides/risk-free-trade-with-slx)**
Move the stop to breakeven automatically once the first target fills, then walk it up behind each
level. Configured once, in the UI or in the payload.

---

## Getting funded

**[Fund Your Account from Any Chain](/docs/guides/fund-from-any-chain)**
Deposit whatever you already hold, on whichever of 56 blockchain networks it sits on, and have it arrive as
USDC margin — no manual bridging, no gas token.
