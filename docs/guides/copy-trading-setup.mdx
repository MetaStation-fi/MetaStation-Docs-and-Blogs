# Set Up Copy Trading Safely

**The job:** follow a provider whose trades replicate into your own account automatically, sized to
your balance and stopped by limits you chose in advance.

**Time:** about 10 minutes. **You need:** a funded account the provider can be copied into — a
MetaStation Account with **Enable Trading** done, or, for a Signal Provider, an account on that
provider's exchange.

---

## Step 1 — Evaluate the provider on more than the return

Open **Social Trade → Providers** and look past the headline. The list is already ranked by a
risk-adjusted **score**, not by raw return, but four things still predict your experience best:

| Look at | Why |
|---|---|
| **Maximum drawdown** | The worst peak-to-trough fall — the number you have to sit through |
| **Track record length** | Three profitable months cannot separate skill from a favourable market |
| **Labels** | *High leverage*, *Returns from a few days*, *Same basket as others*, *Thin liquidity* — read the cautionary ones |
| **What followers made** | Shown once at least 5 people have copied |

Check which **kind** you are looking at — Strategy, Fund Manager or Signal Provider. It changes what
you pay and which account you copy into, not how the trades arrive.

→ [Browse Providers](/docs/social-trading/browse-marketplace) · [How Providers Are Scored](/docs/social-trading/scoring)

## Step 2 — Check the price

- A **Signal Provider** charges a monthly price, prepaid in the Store — or nothing. Your price stays
  what it was when you subscribed
- A **Fund Manager** takes a share of profit, only when a copy exits in profit
- A **Strategy** is copied as an index, which may carry a performance fee on profit only

## Step 3 — Copy into an account of its own

Press **Subscribe** or **Copy**, and under **Copy into** pick the account the copies execute in.

**Use a separate account from your manual trading if you can.** Copied and hand-placed positions
share margin, and separate accounts keep the accounting legible — you can see what the provider
actually made you. The account cannot be changed later.

## Step 4 — Size each copy to your balance

| Mode | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **A share of each trade** | Almost always. A percentage of the provider's size, 0.01%–100% |
| **A fixed amount per trade** | You want every copy to be the same dollar size |
| **The same size as the provider** | Your account is genuinely comparable in size to theirs |

> A provider trading a $100,000 account puts $5,000 into a position. That is 5% of their account.
> Copied at the same size into your $5,000 account, it is **100% of yours**.

For a share, start from `(your balance / provider balance) × 100`, then **halve it** while you learn
how the provider behaves. The sizing mode is fixed once you subscribe.

## Step 5 — Set the limits that stop copying for you

These are the controls that matter most, and they are all on the **Risk** step:

| Setting | Starting value |
|---|---|
| **Stop copying after a loss of** | An amount you would genuinely accept losing on this provider |
| **When it stops, the copied positions are** | **Closed** |
| **Stop for the day after losing** | A fraction of the loss limit |
| **At most this many open positions** | 3–5 |
| **Copy only trades that come with a stop-loss** | On |
| **Leverage** | **My account's leverage**, set to something you are comfortable with — or **Set my own** |
| **Margin mode** | Isolated |

Then decide **what** is copied: only the markets you want, long only or short only, trading hours,
and skip markets too thin to copy well.

→ [Copy Settings](/docs/social-trading/copy-settings)

## Step 6 — Confirm, then watch Activity

Read the summary, tick the risk disclosure, and press **Subscribe**. Copying starts with the
provider's next trade.

**My copies → Activity** lists each copy — including those **made smaller** or **not copied** by your
limits, with the reason. Check it after the first few trades.

---

## Keep the whole book in view

- No more than **30%** of total capital following any one provider.
- Several providers trading **the same basket** are one bet, not a diversified book.
- If a provider goes wrong before your loss limit fires, **Pause** from My copies. Pausing stops new
  copies; it does not close the positions you already hold.

---

## What to expect once it is running

Your results **will not** match the provider's published performance: fills, slippage, latency,
volatility, a different market, and your size all change the outcome — and you joined mid-stream.
