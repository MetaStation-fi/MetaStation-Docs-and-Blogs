# Set Up Copy Trading Safely

**The job:** follow a provider whose trades replicate onto your account automatically, sized to your
balance by a rule you chose in advance.

**Time:** about 10 minutes. **You need:** a funded slot. Copy trading as a follower is free on every
slot type, including the free manual exchange slots.

:::warning[Read this before the steps]
There is **no automatic stop-loss on a subscription**. Nothing pauses copying after a drawdown, and
there is no per-symbol filter and no per-trade cap. Your risk controls are the **copy mode**, the
**leverage**, and **how much you allocate** — all chosen before you subscribe. After that, the only
lever is pausing the subscription yourself.
:::

---

## Step 1 — Evaluate the provider on more than the return

Open the **Marketplace** and look past the headline percentage. Four things predict your experience
better than the return does:

| Look at | Why |
|---|---|
| **Maximum drawdown** | The worst peak-to-trough fall. This is the number you have to sit through — a 200% return with a 60% drawdown is unfollowable for most people |
| **Track record length** | Three profitable months is a sample too small to separate skill from a favourable market |
| **Trade frequency and size consistency** | A steady record punctuated by occasional huge positions describes a strategy that will eventually take one of those on your balance too |
| **Symbols traded** | You will copy all of them. Illiquid pairs also cost more in slippage for a follower, because you arrive after the provider |

Check which **type** of provider you are looking at while you are there — signal provider or fund
manager. It changes what you pay, not how the trades arrive.

→ [Browse the Marketplace](/docs/social-trading/browse-marketplace)
→ [Fund Managers](/docs/social-trading/fund-managers)

## Step 2 — Subscribe on a slot of its own

Subscribe from the provider's card — **Subscribe** on a signal provider, **Allocate** on a fund
manager — and pick the slot the copied trades should execute on.

**Use a separate slot from your manual trading if you can.** Copied and hand-placed positions share
margin, and a copied trade opening while a manual one is running can leave less room than either
strategy assumed. Separate slots also keep the accounting legible — you can see what the provider
actually made you.

→ [Subscribe to a Provider](/docs/social-trading/subscribe-provider)

## Step 3 — Choose the copy mode, and size it to your balance

This is the step people get wrong, and it is the expensive one. It is also **permanent**: the copy
mode cannot be changed after you subscribe. Changing it means cancelling and subscribing again.

| Mode | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **Fixed Ratio** | Almost always. Takes a percentage of the provider's size, 0.01%–100% |
| **Fixed Amount** | You want every copied trade to be the same size regardless of what they risked |
| **Copy Trader** | Your account is genuinely comparable in size to theirs |

Mirroring a provider exactly takes the same **notional** they do — a completely different **risk**
if your accounts differ in size.

> A provider trading a $100,000 account puts $5,000 into a position. That is 5% of their account.
> Mirrored into your $5,000 account, that same $5,000 position is **100% of yours**.

For Fixed Ratio, start from:

```
ratio % = (your balance / provider balance) × 100
```

That reproduces their risk as a percentage of your account. Then **halve it** while you are still
learning how the provider behaves. Scaling up is how a good provider produces a bad outcome for a
follower — and above 100% is not an option, because the ratio is capped there.

## Step 4 — Set leverage and margin deliberately

Under **Advanced Settings**:

| Setting | Range | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **Leverage** | 1× – 125× | No higher than the provider's own. It multiplies the position, not the edge |
| **Margin mode** | Isolated or Cross | Isolated confines a loss to that position's margin |

If the provider trades on **ByBit** you can also override their take profit and stop loss with your
own percentages. The fields are hidden rather than ignored for providers on a MetaStation Account,
so the form tells you whether they apply.

→ [Copy Settings](/docs/social-trading/copy-settings)

## Step 5 — Size the allocation, because it is your real stop

With no automatic circuit breaker, the amount you allocate **is** the maximum you have decided to
risk on this provider. Treat it that way:

- No more than **30%** of total capital to any one provider.
- Give a new provider a small allocation for a full month before increasing it.
- Check whether several providers you follow trade the **same symbols in the same direction** — if
  they do, you have one position spread across several subscriptions, not a diversified book.
- Put a recurring reminder in your calendar to review the subscription. Nothing on the platform is
  going to tap you on the shoulder.

If a provider goes wrong, you pause or cancel from **My Subscriptions**. Pausing stops new copies;
it does **not** close the positions you already hold.

## What to expect once it is running

Your results **will not** match the provider's published performance, for reasons that are
structural rather than anyone's fault:

- Your entry price is later than theirs.
- Your copy mode makes your book a different shape from theirs, not a scaled copy.
- Positions open when you subscribed were not copied — you joined mid-stream.
- Fees and funding accrue at your size.

A provider up 40% means the strategy returned 40% under their sizing and their full trade sequence.
It is not a forecast of your account.
