# Auto-Execute Telegram Signals

**The job:** a signal is posted in a Telegram channel, and the trade opens on your account
automatically — parsed from the message, sized by your rules, not the caller's.

**Time:** about 10 minutes. **You need:** a Telegram account, access to the signal channel, and a
slot with automation (free on your MetaStation Account).

:::caution You are automating someone else's judgement
This is the highest-trust thing you can wire into a trading account. A channel that occasionally
posts an oversized "high conviction" call will act on that conviction with your balance unless you
have capped it. Step 4 is not optional here — do it before mapping any channel.
:::

---

## Step 1 — Connect your Telegram account

Open **Webhook Management** and switch to the **Telegram Connection** tab
([metastation.fi/social-trade/webhook-management](https://metastation.fi/social-trade/webhook-management)).

1. Enter your **phone number with country code** — for example `+1 234 567 8900`.
2. Click **Connect Telegram**.
3. Telegram sends a code to your Telegram app. Enter it under **Enter Verification Code**.
4. If your *Telegram* account has two-step verification enabled, you are asked for that password
   next. That is Telegram's password, not your MetaStation one.

{/* SCREENSHOT: guide-tg-connect | Telegram Connection tab, phone number entry state | 1440x900 | redact: phone number */}

:::warning
Your Telegram session is stored encrypted and MetaStation does not read your private messages.
Never share a Telegram login code with anyone — MetaStation support will never ask for it.
:::

## Step 2 — Let MetaStation detect the channel

Channels are detected **by forwarding**, not by pasting a link:

1. Open the signal channel in Telegram.
2. Forward any message from it to **[@MetaStationBot](https://t.me/MetaStationBot)**.
3. It appears in MetaStation as a detected channel.

The connection card shows how many channels have been detected and how many are set to
copy-trade or broadcast. Press **Refresh** if a newly forwarded channel has not appeared yet.

## Step 3 — Map the channel to an account slot

1. Open the account card for the slot you want to trade on.
2. Pick the detected channel in its Telegram section.
3. Choose whether it is a **copy-trade** source or a **broadcast** target.
4. Save.

:::note Three channels per account
Up to **3 signal channels per account**. If you follow more sources than that, map different
channels to different slots.
:::

## Step 4 — Cap what the channel can do

Set these on the channel before you let it trade. They override whatever the signal says.

| Control | Why it matters here |
|---|---|
| **Position size** | Fixed USD per signal. The caller decides direction; you decide exposure |
| **Max leverage** | A `50x` call executes at your cap instead. This is the setting that separates a loss from a liquidation |
| **Symbol whitelist** | Confines the channel to pairs you actually want, however far it drifts |
| **Max open positions** | Contains a channel that posts ten calls in an hour |

A defensible starting point for an unproven channel: fixed size at 1–2% of balance, leverage
capped at 3x–5x, two or three whitelisted symbols, three open positions maximum.

→ [Risk Controls](/docs/concepts/risk-controls)

## Step 5 — Watch it parse before you trust it

MetaStation reads ordinary signal formatting. A message like this parses cleanly:

```
📈 LONG BTC/USDT
Entry: Market
TP1: 48000
TP2: 51000
SL: 41000
Leverage: 10x
```

The parser extracts action, symbol, direction, entry, take-profit levels, stop loss and leverage.

**If a signal is ambiguous or missing something required, it logs a parse error and executes
nothing.** It never guesses — a wrong trade costs more than a missed one.

Let a channel run for a few real signals with parsing visible before you size up. Channels that
post prose commentary, images, or edits to earlier calls will produce parse errors, and it is
better to learn that at 1% than at 10%.

## Step 6 — Monitor and disconnect

**Webhook Management → Telegram Connection** shows every received message per channel, its parse
result, execution status and trade details.

**Disconnect** ends the session and signal parsing stops immediately. Reconnect any time by
repeating the phone-number flow.

## When a signal does not trade

| Symptom | Cause |
|---|---|
| Channel never appeared | The forward did not reach @MetaStationBot. Forward again, then press Refresh |
| No slot available to map | The target slot has no automation entitlement — free exchange slots need a Store subscription |
| Message logged with a parse error | The format was not understood. Commentary and image-only posts do not parse |
| Parsed but not executed | A risk control blocked it — leverage cap, whitelist, or max open positions |
| Traded smaller than the call | Your position-size setting overrode it, which is the intended behaviour |
