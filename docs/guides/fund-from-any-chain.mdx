# Fund Your Account from Any Chain

**The job:** you hold something, somewhere — ETH on Base, USDT on TRON, SOL on Solana — and you
want it to become trading margin without doing the bridging yourself.

**Time:** minutes, mostly waiting on the source chain.

---

## What you do not have to do

Worth stating, because it is the usual friction elsewhere: you do not need to acquire a specific
token on a specific chain first. No manual bridge, no separate swap, no holding a gas token on
the destination network.

Send what you have. It arrives as USDC.

---

## Step 1 — Open Deposit

**Deposit** in the sidebar — [metastation.fi/deposit](https://metastation.fi/deposit).

Make sure the **MetaStation Account slot** is selected. A connected Binance, ByBit or KuCoin slot
has no Funding Wallet and no deposit address here; you fund those at the exchange itself.

## Step 2 — Pick your source network and asset

Choose the network your funds are on now, and the asset you are sending.

{/* SCREENSHOT: guide-deposit-picker | Deposit screen with network and asset picker open | 1440x900 | redact: Funding Wallet address */}

**56 blockchain networks** are supported, EVM and non-EVM — Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base,
Polygon, BNB Chain, Avalanche, Solana, Bitcoin, Tron, TON, XRP Ledger and more.

→ [Full list: Cross-Chain Exchange](/docs/wallet/cross-chain-exchange)

:::danger Match both, not just one
The most common failed deposit is the right asset on the wrong network — USDT sent over BNB Smart
Chain when Ethereum was selected, or the reverse. The asset and the network must both match what
you chose on screen. Getting this wrong is not recoverable by support in the way an ordinary
mistake would be.
:::

## Step 3 — Send

Send from your wallet or exchange to the address shown.

Whatever you send is bridged into your **Funding Wallet** — a smart-contract wallet on Arbitrum
created with your account — and converted to USDC.

**Send a small test amount first** if this is your first deposit from a given wallet, or if the
amount is large. The cost of a test transfer is trivial against the cost of discovering a mistake
with the whole balance.

## Step 4 — Wait for the source chain

The waiting step is confirmation on the network you sent *from*, not anything on MetaStation's
side. Ethereum takes longer than Arbitrum; that is the chain, not the platform.

Your USDC balance updates once bridging completes, and it is immediately available as margin.

{/* SCREENSHOT: guide-deposit-confirm | deposit confirmation / pending state | 1440x900 | redact: address, amount */}

---

## If it has not arrived

Work through these in order:

1. **Is the transaction finalised on the source chain?** Check the explorer for that network. If
   it is still pending there, the wait is there.
2. **Did the asset and network both match?** Compare what you sent against what the deposit screen
   asked for.
3. **Is the right slot selected?** A MetaStation Account slot has a Funding Wallet; a connected
   exchange slot does not.
4. **Check the deposit screen for status** before contacting support — it will usually tell you
   which stage the deposit is at.

---

## After funding

Your USDC balance is your margin. Profit and loss settle in USDC, so there is no second asset
whose price movement quietly changes your margin health while a position is open.

You never need a gas token: gas on your trades is abstracted away.

→ [Funding and Settlement](/docs/concepts/funding-and-settlement) · [Deposit](/docs/wallet/deposit)

:::note Set up 2FA before you need to withdraw
Withdrawals require your Google Authenticator code **and** an emailed code. Two-factor
authentication is a hard requirement, not a recommendation — and the worst time to set it up is
when you are already trying to withdraw.

→ [Two-Factor Authentication](/docs/security/two-factor-auth)
:::
