# Fund Your Account from Any Chain

**The job:** you hold something, somewhere — USDC on Arbitrum, ETH on Base, SOL on Solana — and you
want it to become trading margin without doing the bridging yourself.

**Time:** minutes, mostly waiting on the source chain.

---

## Pick your route

| You hold… | Route | What you need |
|---|---|---|
| **USDC on Arbitrum One** | **Send manually** to your Funding Wallet address | Any wallet or exchange |
| **A coin on another EVM network, Solana or TON** | **Connect a wallet** — MetaStation bridges it | That network's wallet (EVM, Solana or TON) |
| **BTC, ETH, SOL and other coins, on their own network** (after activation) | Deposit to the **Trading Account** | Any wallet or exchange |

No manual bridge, no separate swap, no holding a gas token on the destination network.

---

## Step 1 — Open Deposit

**Deposit** in the sidebar — [metastation.fi/deposit](https://metastation.fi/deposit).

Under **Deposit to**, pick the **MetaStation Account** you are funding, and **Funding Wallet**.

If this is the account's **first deposit**, the page shows the **one-time activation fee** and the
**minimum** the first deposit must meet. The fee is taken from the deposit; the rest becomes your
trading balance.

## Step 2 — Pick how you send

Under **Send from**:

- **Send manually** — copy the Funding Wallet address and send **USDC on Arbitrum One** to it from any
  wallet or exchange
- **Connect a wallet** — pick the **network you are sending from** and the coin, then connect your
  wallet

:::danger[The manual address takes USDC on Arbitrum One only]
Anything else sent by hand to the Funding Wallet address — another token, or USDC on another
network — is lost. For every other network, use **Connect a wallet**.
:::

## Step 3 — Review and send

With **Connect a wallet**, enter the amount and press **Review deposit**. The review shows what
reaches your Funding Wallet after the route's costs. Confirm, then approve in your wallet — the
network fee is paid in your wallet's gas token on the source chain.

**Send a small test amount first** if this is your first deposit from a given wallet, or if the
amount is large.

## Step 4 — Wait for the source chain

The waiting step is confirmation on the network you sent *from*. Ethereum takes longer than
Arbitrum; that is the chain, not the platform.

The page updates when the deposit arrives — you can leave and come back. On a first deposit it
says *"Deposit received. Your account is activated."*

---

## If it has not arrived

Work through these in order:

1. **Is the transaction finalised on the source chain?** Check the explorer for that network.
2. **Did you send by hand?** Only USDC on Arbitrum One is credited to the Funding Wallet address.
3. **Is it under Deposits in progress?** A bridged deposit shows there until it lands. If it is
   delayed on the network, **Refund delayed deposit** sends it back to your wallet.
4. **Was the first deposit below the minimum?** The page showed the minimum before you sent.
5. Then contact support, with the transaction hash.

---

## After funding

Before your first order, press **Enable Trading** on the account — a one-time step.

Your USDC balance is your margin. Profit and loss settle in USDC.

→ [Funding and Settlement](/docs/concepts/funding-and-settlement) · [Deposit](/docs/wallet/deposit)

:::note[Turn on an authenticator before you need to withdraw]
Every withdrawal is confirmed with a 6-digit code. Without an authenticator app the code comes by
e-mail, and covers at most $1,000 of withdrawals in 24 hours.

→ [Two-Factor Authentication](/docs/security/two-factor-auth)
:::
