# Create Your Account

## Registration

1. Visit [metastation.fi](https://metastation.fi) and click **Get Started**
2. Enter a valid email address and a strong password (min. 8 characters, mixed case + numbers)
3. Check your inbox and click the verification link
4. You are logged in — your **MetaStation Account** is active immediately

No KYC is required to start trading on your MetaStation Account.

---

## What you get on registration

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| MetaStation Account | Native trading account — spot and perpetuals, gasless execution |
| Platform Wallet | For depositing and withdrawing supported assets |
| Free manual slots | 1 free slot each for Binance, ByBit, KuCoin (connect your own API keys) |

---

## Google Sign-In

You can also register and log in with your Google account. Click **Continue with Google** on the login page — no separate password needed.
