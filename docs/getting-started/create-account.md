# Create Your Account

There are three ways in. Each creates the same account, with a **MetaStation Account** ready to
fund.

| Sign in with | Best for |
|---|---|
| **E-mail and password** | Signing in from any browser |
| **Continue with Google** | No separate password |
| **Continue with Telegram** | Using MetaStation inside Telegram, or signing in with your Telegram wallet |

---

## E-mail and password

{/* SCREENSHOT: register-form | /register filled to just-before-submit | 1440x900 | redact: email */}
<Screenshot id="register-form" />

1. Go to [metastation.fi/register](https://metastation.fi/register) — or click **Sign Up Now** on the [home page](https://metastation.fi)
2. Enter your name, a valid e-mail address and a password:
   - **8 to 16 characters**
   - at least one **upper-case** letter, one **lower-case** letter, one **number** and one
     **special character**
3. Accept the terms and press **Create Account**
4. Check your inbox and click the verification link
5. You are signed in — your **MetaStation Account** is ready to fund

{/* SCREENSHOT: verify-email | post-registration verification-pending state | 1440x900 | redact: email */}
<Screenshot id="verify-email" />

Already registered? Sign in at [metastation.fi/login](https://metastation.fi/login).

## Continue with Telegram

On the [login page](https://metastation.fi/login), press **Continue with Telegram**, then choose:

- **Telegram account** — sign in with your Telegram account
- **Telegram wallet** — connect your Telegram wallet and sign in with it

Opening MetaStation from our Telegram bot, [@MetaStationBot](https://t.me/MetaStationBot), signs
you in automatically with your Telegram account.

An account created with Telegram has no e-mail or password at first. To sign in on the web without
Telegram later, open **Profile** and add a sign-in e-mail: we send a code to that address, and you
set a password when you confirm it.

→ [MetaStation in Telegram](/docs/getting-started/telegram)

## What you get on registration

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| MetaStation Account | Your default account — a **Web3 account**. Spot and perpetual futures |
| Funding Wallet | Where your deposits land. Created for you; no seed phrase to manage |
| Exchange slots | Add Binance, ByBit and KuCoin slots with your own API keys whenever you like |

:::info[Your MetaStation Account is Web3 by default]
You do not choose between a "normal" and a "Web3" account. The MetaStation Account **is** a Web3
account: it trades in USDC on-chain and is funded through your **Funding Wallet**. Its first deposit
activates it and pays a one-time activation fee, shown on the Deposit page before you send. See
[Fund your account](/docs/getting-started/fund-account).
:::
