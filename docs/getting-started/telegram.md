# MetaStation in Telegram

MetaStation runs inside Telegram as well as in the browser. It is the same app and the same
account — your slots, balances, copies and automation are all there.

---

## Open it from the bot

1. Open **[@MetaStationBot](https://t.me/MetaStationBot)** in Telegram
2. Press **Open MetaStation** (the button next to the message box), or **Start** the first time
3. MetaStation opens inside Telegram and **signs you in with your Telegram account** — no form,
   no password

If you have never used MetaStation, an account is created for you on the spot, with a MetaStation
Account ready to fund.

:::tip[Press Start once]
Pressing **Start** in the bot also lets it send you messages — for example the 6-digit code that
confirms a withdrawal on an account that has no e-mail address. If you block the bot, those
messages cannot reach you.
:::

---

## Sign in with Telegram on the web

On [metastation.fi/login](https://metastation.fi/login), press **Continue with Telegram**, then
pick one:

| Option | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Telegram account** | Telegram asks you to confirm, and you are signed in with your Telegram account |
| **Telegram wallet** | Connect your Telegram wallet and sign in with it. The wallet list leads with Telegram's own wallets |

Either way it is the same account you use inside Telegram.

---

## Add an e-mail to sign in without Telegram

An account created with Telegram has no e-mail or password at first. To sign in on the web without
Telegram:

1. Open **Profile**
2. Enter the e-mail address you want to sign in with — we send a code to it
3. Enter the code and choose a password

From then on you can sign in with that e-mail and password as well as with Telegram. Adding an
e-mail also means withdrawal confirmation codes can be sent by e-mail rather than by the bot.

---

## What works in Telegram

Everything you can do in the browser: trading, deposits and withdrawals, copy trading, Social
Trade, and automation settings. Withdrawals are confirmed with a 6-digit code like anywhere else —
see [Withdrawal Security](/docs/security/withdrawal-whitelist).

:::note[Telegram-to-Trade is a different thing]
Opening MetaStation in Telegram is about using the app. **Telegram-to-Trade** — executing signals
posted in Telegram channels — is set up separately, on the **Automation** page.
→ [Telegram-to-Trade](/docs/automation/telegram-to-trade)
:::
