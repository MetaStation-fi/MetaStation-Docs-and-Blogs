# Platform Overview

A tour of the MetaStation interface.

---

## Dashboard and Explore

- **Dashboard** — your portfolio at a glance: balances across your accounts, open positions and
  unrealised P&L, recent activity, and the latest announcements
- **Explore** — the markets: prices, movers and the full markets table

---

## Account Slots

Account slots are the core unit of MetaStation. Each slot represents one trading account.

| Slot Type | How to get it | What's included free |
|---|---|---|
| MetaStation Account | Created at registration; add more in Account Management | All automation tools (webhooks, Telegram-to-Trade, copy trading, advanced orders) |
| Binance | Connect your Binance API keys | Manual trading and following providers |
| ByBit | Connect your ByBit API keys | Manual trading and following providers |
| KuCoin | Connect your KuCoin API keys | Manual trading and following providers |

To unlock **automation tools** (webhooks, Telegram-to-Trade) on a Binance, ByBit, or KuCoin slot,
buy a plan for that slot in the **Store**.

→ [Account Slots reference](/docs/trading/account-slots)

---

## Trading Terminal

**Trade** (spot) and **Future** (perpetual futures) open the trading terminal:

- **TradingView chart** with full indicator and drawing tool support
- **Order book** with real-time depth
- **Order ticket** — market, limit and stop orders with take-profit and stop-loss. On a connected
  exchange account it also shows **Your fee rate — Maker / Taker**
- **Open positions** and **open orders**, with **Cancel All** for the pair — it cancels the pair's
  take-profit and stop-loss orders too
- **Order history** and **trade history**; on a MetaStation Account's futures, **Funding Payments**;
  on ByBit futures, **Closed P&L**

Switch between account slots using the account selector at the top of the terminal.

## Navigation

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Your portfolio at a glance |
| **Explore** | Markets and movers |
| **Trade** / **Future** | The trading terminal, spot and perpetual futures |
| **Buy Crypto** | Swap, or pay in a supported fiat currency |
| **Social Trade** | **Discover**, **Indexes**, **Providers**, **My copies**, **My lists** and the **Provider hub** |
| **Account Management** | Your slots, their API keys and their settings |
| **Automation** | Webhook URLs, Trade Settings, Telegram channels and signal history |
| **Wallet** | Balances, and **Transfer** between wallets and accounts |
| **Deposit** / **Withdraw** | Move funds in and out of your MetaStation Account |
| **History** | Orders, trades, deposits, withdrawals, funding payments |
| **Profile** | Your details, sign-in e-mail, and public trader profile |
| **Security** | Authenticator, password, Sign out everywhere, dead man's switch |
| **Referrals** | Your referral link and codes |
| **Store** | Plans for exchange slots, paid in USDC |
| **Accessibility** | Display and motion preferences for this device |
| **Support** | Contact support |

Withdrawals from a connected Binance, ByBit or KuCoin account are made on the exchange itself;
the Withdraw page links you there.
