# Quick Start

Get from zero to your first trade in a few minutes.

---

## Step 1 — Create your account

1. Go to [metastation.fi/register](https://metastation.fi/register) — or click **Sign Up Now** on the home page
2. Register with your e-mail and a password, or use **Continue with Google** or **Continue with
   Telegram**
3. Verify your e-mail address if you registered with one
4. Your **MetaStation Account** is created automatically — no exchange setup required. It is a
   **Web3 account by default**, funded through your Funding Wallet

:::tip
Turn on an authenticator app straight after registration. It protects every sign-in, and without it
withdrawals are capped at $1,000 a day. See [Security Setup](/docs/getting-started/security-setup).
:::

---

## Step 2 — Fund your account

{/* SCREENSHOT: deposit-picker | reuse of the fund-account capture | 1440x900 | redact: address */}
<Screenshot id="deposit-picker" />

1. Open **Deposit** in the sidebar ([metastation.fi/deposit](https://metastation.fi/deposit))
2. Pick your MetaStation Account and its **Funding Wallet**
3. Either **Send manually** — USDC on Arbitrum One, from any wallet or exchange — or **Connect a
   wallet** on another network and MetaStation bridges it for you
4. Your **first deposit** pays a one-time activation fee (shown on the page, with the minimum) and
   activates the account

No crypto yet? **Buy Crypto** in the top navigation opens the Crypto Swap page, where you can
swap into a supported asset or pay with a supported fiat currency through SimpleSwap.

→ Full guide: [Fund Your Account](/docs/getting-started/fund-account)

---

## Step 3 — Place your first trade

{/* SCREENSHOT: first-trade | trade terminal, order ticket filled but NOT submitted | 1440x900 | redact: balance */}
<Screenshot id="first-trade" />

1. Open **Trade** (spot) or **Future** (perpetual futures)
2. Select your account (MetaStation Account is active by default)
3. The first time, press **Enable Trading** on the order ticket — a one-time step per account
4. Pick a trading pair — e.g. `BTCUSDC`
5. Set your order type, size, and optional Take Profit / Stop Loss
6. Click **Buy / Long** or **Sell / Short**

:::note
Orders on the MetaStation Account cost no network gas.
:::
