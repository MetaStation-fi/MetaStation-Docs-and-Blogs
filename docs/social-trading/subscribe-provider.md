# Subscribe to a Provider

Once you find a provider you want to follow, subscribe to start copying their trades.

---

## How to subscribe

1. Open the provider's profile in the **Marketplace**
2. Click **Subscribe**
3. Select a plan: Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual
4. Choose which account slot will copy the trades
5. Confirm payment — copying begins immediately

---

## Subscription plans

Most providers offer multiple billing periods. Quarterly and annual plans are typically discounted.

Subscriptions are charged from your MetaStation wallet balance. Ensure your wallet has sufficient funds before subscribing.

---

## What happens after subscribing

- The provider's trades are replicated to your selected account slot in real time
- Your position sizes are calculated based on your copy settings (see [Copy Settings](/docs/social-trading/copy-settings))
- You can monitor copied positions in the **Portfolio** tab alongside your manual positions

---

## Pause and resume

You can pause copying at any time without cancelling your subscription:

1. Go to **Marketplace → My Subscriptions**
2. Click **Pause** next to the provider
3. Click **Resume** when ready

Pausing stops new trades from being copied. Existing open positions are not affected.

---

## Cancel a subscription

1. Go to **Marketplace → My Subscriptions**
2. Click **Cancel** next to the provider
3. Confirm cancellation

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Existing copied positions remain open and must be closed manually.

---

## Following multiple providers

You can subscribe to multiple providers simultaneously. Each subscription can be assigned to the same or different account slots. Diversifying across 3–5 providers with different strategies reduces correlation risk.
