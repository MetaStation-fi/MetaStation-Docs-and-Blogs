# Browse Providers and Strategies

**Social Trade → Providers** ([metastation.fi/social-trade/providers](https://metastation.fi/social-trade/providers))
lists everyone you can copy, ranked by a published score rather than by raw return. You can browse
it without signing in; copying asks you to sign in and brings you back.

## Three kinds, one list

| Kind | Who they are | How they are paid | Copied into |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strategies** | Tracked on-chain trading records MetaStation scores | A performance fee on profit only | A MetaStation Account, as its own single-strategy index |
| **Fund Managers** | Users who trade their own account and let others copy it | A **share of profit**, only when a copy exits in profit | A MetaStation Account |
| **Signal Providers** | Users who publish signals from a webhook or a Telegram channel | A **monthly price**, prepaid, or free | A MetaStation Account, or an account on the provider's own exchange |

Use the **Kind** chips — **All**, **Strategies**, **Fund Managers**, **Signal Providers** — to narrow
the list. In every case the trades are copied into **your own account**; MetaStation does not manage
your funds.

The old **Marketplace** and **Leaderboard** links now open this page.

## Sort and rails

| Sort | What it ranks by |
|---|---|
| **Score** | The overall 0–100 score — the default |
| **Return (risk-adjusted)** | 90-day return against the risk taken to earn it |
| **Lowest drawdown** | The shallowest losses from a high |
| **Consistency** | How steady the gains are |
| **Capacity** | How much capital can copy it before the copies move the market |
| **Tracked longest** | The longest record |

There is no sort by raw return. **Rails** give a quick cut: **Every provider**, **Best for
subscribers**, **Lowest drawdown**, **Most followed**, **New**.

## Filters

| Filter | Options |
|---|---|
| **Exchange** | MetaStation Account, or Exchange account |
| **Fee** | Free or Paid |
| **Grade** | A or better, B or better, C or better |
| **Max drawdown at most** | 10%, 20%, 30% |
| **Trades a week at least** | 1, 5, 20 |
| **Average trade** | Under a day, 1–7 days, Over a week |
| **Subscribers at least** | 5, 20 |
| **Active within** | 7 days, 30 days |
| **Tracked** | 30 days, 90 days, 180 days, 1 year |
| **Capacity** | $100k, $1M, $10M |

Signed in, you can save a set of filters and come back to it.

## Labels

Cards carry plain labels. The cautionary ones are shown as prominently as the good ones:

| Label | Means |
|---|---|
| **High capacity** · **Majors focus** · **Patient** · **Rising** | What kind of record it is |
| **Earns mostly from funding** | Most of the return is funding payments, not price moves |
| **Same basket as others** | Trades the same markets, the same way, as other providers — following both is not diversification |
| **Very short holds** · **High leverage** | Hard or risky to copy |
| **Returns from a few days** | Most of the return came from a handful of days |
| **Unusual volume** · **Short history** · **Not copyable** | Read the profile before copying |
| **Copied on a different market** · **Delayed copy** · **Thin liquidity** | Your copy will differ from the original |

## Fresh performers

A strategy is ranked only once it has **14 days** of tracked record. Newer records with strong early
results appear separately, as **Fresh performers**, each marked **Tracked N days** — a short record
is a story, not a distribution.

## Reading a profile

{/* SCREENSHOT: provider-detail | Provider profile, score, figures and chart | 1440x900 | redact: none */}
<Screenshot id="provider-detail" />

The profile shows the score and grade, risk-adjusted return, drawdown, consistency, trade
frequency, how long the record runs, and — for Signal Providers and Fund Managers — their fee and
what their followers actually made (once at least 5 of them have).

Read the **drawdown before the return**. Check the **trade count**, because twelve trades is a
story and three hundred is a distribution. Check **how long the record runs** — three profitable
months cannot separate skill from a rising market.

The action on a profile is **Subscribe** (Signal Provider) or **Copy** (Fund Manager, or a
strategy whose single-strategy index is open).

---

## Compare, watch and lists

- **Compare** — up to four side by side, from the browse
- **Watch** — keep a provider in view without copying
- **My lists** — group the providers you are considering
- **Baskets** — pick several Signal Providers and Fund Managers and copy them together; preview the
  basket before anything is created, and stop one leg at a time from **My copies → Baskets**
