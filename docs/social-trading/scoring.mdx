# How Providers Are Scored

Every strategy MetaStation tracks is scored from **0 to 100** on **six parts**. Each part compares
its record with every other strategy tracked, so a score says **where it stands**, not how much it
made.

**Return alone never ranks anything.** Performance counts return only against the risk taken to
earn it.

The live method — with the exact weights and inputs — is published at **Social Trade → How the score
works** ([metastation.fi/social-trade/methodology](https://metastation.fi/social-trade/methodology)).

## The six parts

| Part | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Performance** | Risk-adjusted return over 90 days, against every strategy tracked |
| **Drawdown control** | How deep and how long its losses from a high have been |
| **Consistency** | How steady its gains are from day to day and month to month |
| **Copyability** | How well a copy can follow it: holding time, order size, market depth |
| **Risk posture** | How much leverage and concentration it takes |
| **Longevity** | How long it has a record, and whether it is still active |

A ratio is used only with its sample size. A part with no data counts as **50 of 100** — the middle
— and is marked as missing rather than guessed.

---

## Grades

| Grade | Score from |
|---|---|
| **S** | 85 |
| **A** | 75 |
| **B** | 65 |
| **C** | 55 |
| **D** | 40 |

Below 40, a strategy is not graded.

---

## A quiet record counts for less

A record that has stopped trading loses weight: after **2 quiet days** the score is scaled down a
little each day, to at most **half** after about **14 days**.

## Warnings that take points off

| Warning | Effect |
|---|---|
| **High leverage** | The risk-posture part is capped |
| **Trading with itself** | Points are taken off |
| **Results rest on a few large wins** | The whole score is capped |

The exact sizes are on the methodology page.

---

## When a record is ranked

A record needs at least **14 days** of tracking before it is ranked. Newer records with strong early
results show separately as **Fresh performers**, each marked **Tracked N days**.
