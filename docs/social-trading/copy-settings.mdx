# Copy Trading Settings

Copy settings decide one thing: how big your version of the provider's trade is. Everything else
follows from that — the margin it locks up, the share of your account it puts at risk, and how much
the trade has to make before the subscription has paid for itself.

---

## Where they live

**Marketplace → My Subscriptions → Settings**, next to a provider.

:::warning[Copy mode is fixed once you subscribe]
The three modes below are chosen when you subscribe and **cannot be changed afterwards** — the
settings form ignores the change on an existing subscription. Everything else on the page can be
edited at any time. To move from one copy mode to another, cancel and subscribe again.
:::

---

## The three copy modes

| Mode | What it does | Range |
|---|---|---|
| **Copy Trader** | Mirrors the provider's position size exactly | — |
| **Fixed Ratio** | Takes a **percentage of the provider's** position size | 0.01% – 100% |
| **Fixed Amount** | Uses a flat position size on every copied trade | Set by the provider — 100 to 10,000 by default |

**Fixed Ratio is capped at 100%.** You cannot copy more than the provider's own size: a value above
100 fails validation before the subscription is saved. If a provider trades smaller than you want to,
the answer is leverage or a different provider, not the ratio.

**Fixed Amount ignores what the provider risked.** Every copied trade is the same size, whether the
provider staked a tenth of their account or half of it. It is the predictable option and the blunt
one.

**Copy Trader is only sensible if your account is the same size as theirs.** Mirroring a provider
with ten times your balance mirrors their notional, not their risk.

### Sizing Fixed Ratio to your account

The ratio that reproduces the provider's *risk* rather than their notional is the ratio of the two
balances:

```
ratio % = (your balance / provider balance) × 100
```

A 2,000 account following a 20,000 provider is `10%`. That is the starting point, not the answer —
halve it while you are learning how the provider behaves.

## Leverage and margin

Under **Advanced Settings**:

| Setting | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Margin mode** | Isolated or Cross | Isolated confines a loss to that position's margin |
| **Leverage** | 1× – 125× | Applied to your copied position, independent of the provider's |

Leverage multiplies the position, not the edge. At 10× a copied trade needs a tenth of the margin
and moves ten times as fast against you.

---

## Take profit and stop loss overrides

If the provider trades on **ByBit**, you can override their take profit and stop loss with your own
percentages, leaving the rest of the trade as they set it.

These overrides are **not offered for providers trading a MetaStation Account** — that venue does
not accept a follower-side TP/SL override — and the fields are hidden rather than ignored, so you
will know from the form whether they apply.

---

## What you are still responsible for

Copy settings size the trade. They do not watch it.

- **There is no automatic circuit breaker.** Nothing pauses copying for you after a drawdown. If a
  provider is losing, you pause or cancel the subscription yourself, from
  **My Subscriptions → Pause**.
- **There is no per-symbol filter.** Every symbol the provider trades is copied. If you do not want
  their altcoin trades, you do not want the provider.
- **Pausing does not close anything.** Existing copied positions stay open and remain yours to
  manage. → [Subscribe to a Provider](/docs/social-trading/subscribe-provider)

:::tip[A workable starting point]
- Fixed Ratio, at half the balance-derived ratio, until the provider has earned your confidence
- Leverage no higher than the provider's own
- Margin per copied trade at 5–10% of your balance — the calculator above warns past 30%
- No more than 30% of your capital following any one provider
- A weekly look at the subscription, because nothing else is going to take it for you
:::
