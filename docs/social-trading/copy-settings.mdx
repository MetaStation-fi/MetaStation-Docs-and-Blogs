# Copy Trading Settings

Copy settings decide how big your version of the provider's trade is, and when copying stops. They
are set on the provider's **Copy** page, in three steps — **Size**, **Risk**, **Confirm** — and can
be edited later from **My copies → Edit copy settings**.

Copies always execute in **your own account**. MetaStation does not manage your funds, and the
provider never has access to your account.

---

## Size

### Copy or countertrade

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Copy** | Each copy takes the provider's side of the trade |
| **Countertrade** | Each copy takes the **opposite** side: the provider's long is your short |

:::warning[Countertrade]
A countertrade has the provider's timing with the opposite exposure: when the provider's trade
gains, yours loses. Only **market** entries are countered — a limit or stop entry, or a trade
managed with several take-profits, a trailing stop or a break-even rule, is not copied and shows in
your activity with the reason. A countertrade **requires a loss limit** or a trailing stop, and the
choice is **fixed** once you subscribe.
:::

### How each copy is sized

| Mode | What it does | Range |
|---|---|---|
| **A share of each trade** | A **percentage of the provider's** order size | 0.01% – 100% |
| **A fixed amount per trade** | **This many dollars of the coin** on every copy, rounded down to the venue's size step | At least 1 USD, up to the provider's maximum (10,000 by default) |
| **The same size as the provider** | The provider's own order size. Your account needs the margin for it | — |

**A share is capped at 100%.** You cannot copy more than the provider's own size. If a provider
trades smaller than you want to, the answer is leverage or a different provider, not the share.

**A fixed amount ignores what the provider risked.** Every copy is the same dollar size, whether
the provider staked a tenth of their account or half of it. It is the predictable option and the
blunt one.

**The same size is only sensible if your account is the same size as theirs.** Mirroring a provider
with ten times your balance mirrors their notional, not their risk.

### Copy into

The account the copies execute in. Which accounts are offered depends on the provider:

| Provider | Can be copied into |
|---|---|
| **Signal Provider** | A MetaStation Account, or an account on the provider's own exchange |
| **Fund Manager** | A MetaStation Account |

A MetaStation Account must have **Enable Trading** done first. The account cannot be changed after
you subscribe.

The calculator refuses settings the platform refuses, and says which rule they broke. The
break-even figures are the ones worth sitting with: a subscription is a **fixed** monthly cost, so
the smaller your allocation, the larger the return it has to produce before you are ahead of simply
not subscribing.

### Sizing a share to your account

The share that reproduces the provider's *risk* rather than their notional is the ratio of the two
balances:

```
share % = (your balance / provider balance) × 100
```

A 2,000 account following a 20,000 provider is `10%`. That is the starting point, not the answer —
halve it while you are learning how the provider behaves.

## Risk

### Leverage and margin

| Setting | Options | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Whose leverage** | **My account's leverage** (default) or **Set my own** (1×–125×) | The provider's leverage is never used |
| **Margin mode** | **Isolated** or **Cross** | Isolated confines a loss to that position's margin |

With **My account's leverage**, each copy opens at the leverage your account already has for that
market — a change you made by hand stays.

### Stop copying after a loss

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Stop copying after a loss of** (USD) | Counted on this subscription's copies only, realised and open. Your own trades on the account are never counted or closed |
| **Trailing stop** (USD) | Stops after the copies fall this far below their best result since you started or re-armed |
| **When it stops, the copied positions are** | **Closed**, **Kept open**, or **Closed with the provider** (they stay open until the provider closes theirs) |
| **Stop for the day after losing** (USD) | A daily loss limit |

Once a subscription stops, it **stays stopped** until you **re-arm** it from My copies. Its status
reads **Stopped by your loss limit**.

### What is copied

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Markets** | **All markets**, **Only these**, or **All but these** (for example `BTC, ETH`) |
| **Direction** | **Both**, **Long only** or **Short only** |
| **At most this many open positions** | A new market waits until one closes; adding to one you hold still copies |
| **Minutes between openings in a market** | A cooldown after each copy opens |
| **Most margin in one market** (USD) | A copy that would pass it is not opened |
| **Most in open copies, all markets** (USD) | The value of every open copy together; a copy that would pass it is not opened |
| **Copy only trades that come with a stop-loss** | A signal without one is not opened |
| **Skip markets priced below** / **that traded less in 24 hours than** | Avoids illiquid markets |
| **Copy only between** (hours) | Openings outside these hours, in your time zone, are not copied |
| **Stop opening copies after** (a date) | Nothing new opens after that day |

Filters apply to **openings only**. A close always copies, so a position is never stranded by a
filter you set later.

### Take profit, stop loss and entries

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Take profit** / **Stop loss** | Each copy gets its own, as a return on its margin (ROI %) or in dollars. It **replaces** the provider's |
| **Cancel an entry still waiting after** (minutes) | A copy's limit entry that has not filled by then is cancelled |
| **Worst entry against the provider's price** (%) | A copy is not opened at a price worse than this |

### The provider's settings

A provider can publish **default settings** for their followers. If they have, the copy page shows
them, and you can use them as they are or change any of them.

## Confirm

The last step shows a summary — the provider, copy or countertrade, each copy's size, the account,
leverage and margin, what is copied, and the loss limit — and a risk disclosure you tick. Copying
starts with the provider's **next** trade; positions the provider already holds are not copied.

## After you subscribe

From **My copies** ([metastation.fi/social-trade/portfolio](https://metastation.fi/social-trade/portfolio)):

- **Edit copy settings** — the size, leverage, filters, caps and protection. Copy or countertrade,
  the sizing mode and the account are fixed when you subscribe; to change them, cancel and
  subscribe again
- **Pause** — new trades are not copied; positions already open stay open
- **Resume** / **Re-arm** — copying starts again; a re-armed loss limit counts from now
- **Cancel subscription** — no new trades are copied; open positions stay until you close them

→ [Subscribe to a Provider](/docs/social-trading/subscribe-provider)

:::tip[A workable starting point]
- A share of each trade, at half the balance-derived share, until the provider has earned your
  confidence
- A loss limit you would actually accept losing, with **Closed** as what happens
- **Copy only trades that come with a stop-loss**
- At most 3–5 open positions
- No more than 30% of your capital following any one provider
:::
