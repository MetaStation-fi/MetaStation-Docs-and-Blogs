# Your Public Profile

Every provider can have a public page at **metastation.fi/@handle**. It is how followers find and
share you.

---

## Setting it up

Open **Profile → Public profile**:

| Field | Rules |
|---|---|
| **Handle** | 3 to 20 letters, numbers or underscores. It can change once every 30 days |
| **Display name** | The name shown on your page |
| **Bio** | A short description. No links |

**Your profile goes public once you publish a service** — a Signal Provider or Fund Manager product.
Until then the page says *"This profile is not public."* The page shows a checklist of what is left:
choose a handle, write a bio, add a picture to a service, publish a service.

**View as visitor** shows you the page as others see it.

---

## What it shows

- Your **display name** and **@handle** — never your real name or any identity details
- Your bio
- The services you publish, with their figures and how to copy or subscribe

It does not show a feed of your trades, and there are no comments.

---

## Watching a profile

Visitors can **Watch** a profile to keep it in view — but only if its owner allows it. Watching is
**off by default**; turn it on in your profile settings.

---

## Reporting a profile

Anyone signed in can press **Report this profile** and choose a reason: pretends to be someone else,
misleading claims about results, spam or advertising, an offensive name, bio or picture, or
something else. Reports are reviewed.
