# MetaStation Indexes

An **index** copies a set of scored strategies into **your own account**, by fixed rules that are
published on the index's page. Instead of choosing one trader, you allocate to the rules.

:::note[What an index is, and is not]
MetaStation places an index's orders in your account by the rules shown on its page. **It does not
manage your account.** Whether those orders fit your risk appetite is your decision.
:::

Browse them at **Social Trade → Indexes** ([metastation.fi/social-trade/indexes](https://metastation.fi/social-trade/indexes)).
You can read every index signed out.

---

## Reading an index

Each index page shows:

| Section | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Performance** | The index's record, with **Max drawdown** next to the return |
| **Composition** | The strategies copied, and **How your capital is split** between them |
| **Score, risk and protection** | Risk and return figures, and the protection the index applies |
| **Rules and precautions** | **The rules this index follows**, and **What MetaStation does on every order** |
| **Roster changes** | When strategies joined or left, and why |
| **Performance fee** | What is charged, if anything. *No profit, no fee* |

**Compare** puts indexes side by side, and **How the score works** explains how the strategies in
them were chosen. → [How Providers Are Scored](/docs/social-trading/scoring)

## Single-strategy indexes

A tracked **strategy** is copied as its own index — a **single-strategy index**. A strategy opens to
copying this way once it has finished its time tracked on paper and still passes the checks. Until
then its profile says *"Not open to copy yet"* and points to the list of single-strategy indexes
that are open.

---

## Allocating

Allocating copies the index into one of your MetaStation Accounts:

1. Open the index and press **Allocate**
2. Enter the **Amount (USD)** and pick the account it comes **From**
3. Choose a **Risk profile**, and adjust the settings if you want
4. Read the **Preview** — what would be opened, and anything that would not be copied
5. **Confirm**. You see *"Your allocation is running."*

Each index page says whether allocating is open for it. Where it is not yet, the page says
*"Allocating to indexes is not open yet."*

### After you allocate

From **My copies → Allocations**:

- **Adjust protection** — tighten or loosen the allocation's protection levels
- **Stop** — copying stops, by the rules shown on the index page

### Try it on paper first

Where it is offered, **Try on paper** runs an index with paper money — no real orders — so you can
watch how it behaves before you commit. A paper allocation can later be **converted to real**.

---

## Indexes and your own limits

An index applies your stop-loss, leverage and position-size limits to every copied order. An order
can be made smaller, or skipped, by those limits; **My copies → Activity** says which, and why.
