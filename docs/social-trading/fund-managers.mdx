# Fund Managers

On MetaStation, a **Fund Manager** is a user who trades their own account and lets others copy it.
The name describes how they are paid — a share of profit — not what they do with anyone's money.

:::note[MetaStation does not manage your funds — and neither does a fund manager]
Following a fund manager copies their trades into **your** account, sized by **your** settings. Your
balance never moves to the manager, the manager has no access to your account, and you can stop
copying at any time.
:::

| | Signal Provider | Fund Manager |
|---|---|---|
| **Trades on** | Their own account | **Their own account** — exchange keys they connect, or a MetaStation Account |
| **Their trades reach you via** | Signals from a webhook or a Telegram channel | Their account's trades, copied as they make them |
| **Charges** | A monthly price, prepaid — or nothing | A **share of profit**, only when you profit |
| **You copy into** | A MetaStation Account, or an account on their exchange | A **MetaStation Account** |
| **Button on the profile** | **Subscribe** | **Copy** |

Everything downstream is **identical**. Your sizing, your leverage, your margin mode and your risk
limits behave exactly as they do for a signal provider.

→ [Copy Settings](/docs/social-trading/copy-settings) covers the follower side in full.

---

## The account a fund manager trades

A fund manager picks the account they trade from at signup — an exchange account connected with
their own API keys, or a MetaStation Account. MetaStation does not create, fund or run that account.
The record you see on their profile is that account's trading.

---

## How a fund manager is paid

The **performance fee** is a percentage of the **profit** a follower's copy makes:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Rate** | 0%, or a whole number from 2% to 99% — never above the manager's tier limit (10% New, 15% Bronze, 20% Silver, 30% Gold) |
| **When it is taken** | Only when a copy **exits in profit** — and never while the copy is below its starting value |
| **Measured against** | The copy's high-water mark: you never pay twice for the same profit |
| **Where it is shown** | On the manager's profile and in the copy summary before you confirm |

If a manager's tier falls, the rate you pay is capped at the new limit, and you are told once that
the fee went down.

:::tip[The incentive is the thing to actually think about]
A subscription is paid whether you make money or not. A profit share is only earned when you
profit — which aligns the manager with you on direction, and *misaligns* them on risk, because the
upside of a leveraged bet is shared and the downside is yours. Neither model is safer than the
other. They fail differently.
:::

---

## The leverage range is a description

A fund manager publishes the **leverage range** they trade in. It describes the strategy's
volatility; it does not set the leverage on your copies. Your copies open at **your account's
leverage** for that market, or at the leverage you set — never at the manager's.

---

## Following a fund manager

1. **Social Trade → Providers**, and pick **Fund Managers** in the **Kind** chips.
2. **Read the profile as you would any provider.** Score and grade, drawdown, consistency, trade
   count, record length, and what followers made.
3. **Press Copy.** Choose a MetaStation Account to copy into, size each copy, and set your risk
   limits — including a loss limit that stops copying automatically.
4. **Confirm.** Copying starts with the manager's next trade.

→ [Browse Providers](/docs/social-trading/browse-marketplace)
→ [Subscribe to a Provider](/docs/social-trading/subscribe-provider)

---

## Becoming a fund manager

The provider signup asks which kind you are registering as before anything else; a fund manager
then picks the account they trade from and their performance fee.
