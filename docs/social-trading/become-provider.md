# Become a Provider

Providers earn recurring subscription revenue from followers who copy their trades. Your trades execute normally — followers' accounts replicate them automatically.

---

## Requirements

- Verified MetaStation account in good standing
- Active trading history (3+ months recommended)
- Willingness to maintain transparent, honest communication with subscribers

---

## Application process

1. Go to **Marketplace → Become a Provider**
2. Fill in the application form:
   - Display name and profile photo
   - Trading strategy description (be specific — explain your approach, timeframe, and risk management)
   - Subscription pricing for each tier
   - Risk disclaimer
3. Submit — review takes 2–5 business days
4. On approval, your profile goes live in the marketplace

---

## Setting up your profile

**Strategy description** — Describe exactly what you trade, how, and why. Transparency builds subscriber trust and retention.

Good example:
> "I trade BTC and ETH perpetuals on the 4-hour timeframe using support/resistance levels and volume confirmation. I risk 1–2% per trade with fixed Stop Loss. Average 15–25 trades per month."

Avoid:
> "I trade crypto and make consistent profits." (too vague)

**Subscription tiers** — You can offer monthly, quarterly, and annual plans. Setting a lower entry price helps attract initial subscribers while you build a track record.

---

## Revenue and payouts

You earn subscription fees from your subscribers. MetaStation retains a platform commission; the remainder goes to you.

Payouts are processed on a regular schedule to your MetaStation wallet. Minimum payout thresholds apply — check your Provider Dashboard for current figures.

---

## Managing your provider account

**Provider Dashboard shows:**
- Total active subscribers
- Subscription revenue (current period and all-time)
- Performance metrics (ROI, win rate, drawdown)
- Per-subscriber view (active/paused status)

---

## Responsibilities

:::warning
**As a provider, you are not a registered financial advisor.** You must include a risk disclaimer on your profile. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Subscribers can and will lose money following your trades.

Providers found engaging in misleading practices (inflated screenshots, guaranteed return claims, pump-and-dump schemes) are permanently removed from the platform.
:::
