# Subscribe to a Provider

Subscribing is three decisions: **which slot** executes the copies, **which copy mode** sizes them,
and **how much** you are putting behind it.

---

## Before you start

You need a **funded trading slot** for the copies to execute on. If that slot is a
**MetaStation Web3** account, two prerequisites have to be satisfied first, and the subscription is
refused with a specific message until they are:

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| *Enable trading* | Trading is not yet enabled on that slot |
| *Approve builder fee first* | The builder fee approval has not been granted for that account |

Both are one-time actions on the account, not per-subscription.

---

## Subscribing

1. **Open the provider from the Marketplace** and press the action button — **Subscribe** on a
   signal provider, **Allocate** on a fund manager. Both open the same copy settings.
2. **Choose the slot** the copied trades should execute on.
3. **Choose your copy mode and amount.** Copy Trader, Fixed Ratio (0.01%–100% of the provider's
   size) or Fixed Amount.
4. **Set leverage and margin mode** under Advanced Settings.
5. **Confirm.** Copying starts from the provider's next trade.

:::warning[The copy mode is permanent]
Copy mode is fixed at subscription. The settings form ignores a change to it on an existing
subscription — everything else stays editable. Moving between copy modes means cancelling and
subscribing again, so it is worth being right the first time.
:::

→ [Copy Settings](/docs/social-trading/copy-settings) covers the sizing in full.

**Use a slot separate from your manual trading if you can.** Copied and hand-placed positions share
margin, and separate slots keep the accounting legible.

---

## What the provider's terms mean for you

A provider's commercial terms are shown on their marketplace card and profile:

- A **signal provider** shows a **Fee** — the subscription fee they set, which may be zero.
- A **fund manager** shows a **Profit Share** percentage, between 10% and 30%.

Neither changes how your position is sized. Sizing is entirely your copy mode and your allocation.

→ [Fund Managers](/docs/social-trading/fund-managers)

---

## After it is running

- The provider's trades replicate onto your chosen slot as they happen.
- Only trades opened **after** you subscribe are copied. Positions the provider already held are
  not backfilled — you join mid-strategy.
- Copied positions appear alongside your manual ones on that slot, which is the argument for
  keeping them apart.

A subscription is **active**, **paused** or **cancelled**.

---

## Pause and resume

From **My Subscriptions**, pause next to the provider, and resume when you want it running again.

**Pausing stops new trades being copied. It does not close anything.** Positions already open stay
open and remain yours to manage or close by hand.

Pausing is also the only stop that exists. There is no automatic threshold that pauses copying
after a drawdown, so if a provider is going badly, this is the action — and it is yours to take.

→ [How Copy Trading Executes](/docs/concepts/copy-trading-mechanics)

---

## Cancel

Cancel from the same screen. **Existing copied positions remain open after cancellation** and must
be closed manually — cancelling ends the copying relationship, not your exposure to what it already
opened.

---

## Following more than one provider

You can hold several subscriptions at once, on the same slot or on different ones.

Spreading across several providers only diversifies if they are actually trading differently. Check
whether they take the **same symbols in the same direction** — several subscriptions onto one
correlated bet is a concentrated position wearing a diversified costume, and it is the failure mode
that shows up most often.
