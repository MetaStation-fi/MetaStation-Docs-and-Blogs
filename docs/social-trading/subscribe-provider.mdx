# Subscribe to a Provider

Subscribing copies a provider's future trades into **your own account**, sized and limited by your
own settings. MetaStation does not manage your funds, and the provider never has access to your
account.

---

## Before you start

You need a **funded account** for the copies to execute in, and it must be one the provider can be
copied into:

| Provider | Can be copied into |
|---|---|
| **Signal Provider** | A MetaStation Account, or an account on the provider's own exchange |
| **Fund Manager** | A MetaStation Account |

A tracked **Strategy** is not subscribed to: it is copied as its own single-strategy index, once
that index is open. → [MetaStation Indexes](/docs/social-trading/indexes)

A **MetaStation Account** must have trading enabled first. Until it has, the subscription is
refused with *"Enable trading on your MetaStation account first"* — press **Enable Trading** on the
account once. It is a one-time action, not a per-subscription step.

---

## Subscribing

1. **Open the provider** from **Social Trade → Providers** and press **Subscribe** (a Signal
   Provider) or **Copy** (a Fund Manager)
2. **Plan** — a paid Signal Provider shows its price first (see below)
3. **Size** — **Copy** or **Countertrade**, how each copy is sized, and the account to copy into
4. **Risk** — leverage and margin mode, a loss limit, which markets and direction, caps, take-profit
   and stop-loss
5. **Confirm** — read the summary, tick the risk disclosure, and press **Subscribe**

You see *"Copying has started."* Copying starts with the provider's **next** trade.

→ [Copy Settings](/docs/social-trading/copy-settings) explains every setting.

:::warning[Fixed when you subscribe]
**Copy or countertrade**, the **sizing mode** and the **account** cannot be changed afterwards.
Everything else stays editable from **My copies**. To change one of the three, cancel and subscribe
again.
:::

**Use an account separate from your manual trading if you can.** Copied and hand-placed positions
share margin, and separate accounts keep the accounting legible. Your loss limit only ever counts —
and closes — this subscription's own copies.

---

## Paying a Signal Provider

A Signal Provider sets a **monthly price**, from free to $10,000, with an optional free trial of up
to 30 days. The Plan step says exactly what you pay:

- The month is **paid ahead in the Store**, from a MetaStation Account. Copying starts once the first
  month is paid (or straight away during a free trial)
- **Nothing new is copied in a month that is not paid.** My copies shows **Pay in the Store** /
  **Pay next month** when the next month is due
- **Your price is locked in.** It stays what it was when you subscribed, even if the provider
  changes it later

A **Fund Manager** charges no subscription. They are paid a **share of the profit** their followers
make, taken only when a copy exits in profit, and never while the copy is below its starting value.

→ [Fund Managers](/docs/social-trading/fund-managers)

Some products are **invite only** — you can subscribe only through the invite link the provider
gave you.

---

## After it is running

- The provider's trades replicate onto your chosen account as they happen.
- Only trades opened **after** you subscribe are copied. Positions the provider already held are
  not backfilled — you join mid-strategy.
- **My copies → Activity** lists each copy, including the ones that were **not copied** or **made
  smaller**, with the reason.

A subscription is **Running**, **Paused**, **Stopped by your loss limit**, or **Cancelled**.

---

## Pause, resume and re-arm

From **My copies** ([metastation.fi/social-trade/portfolio](https://metastation.fi/social-trade/portfolio)):

- **Pause** — new trades from this provider are not copied. Positions already open stay open
- **Resume** — new trades are copied again
- **Re-arm** — after your loss limit stopped the subscription, copying starts again and the loss
  limit counts from now

Your **loss limit** and **trailing stop** stop a subscription automatically; what happens to its
open positions — closed, kept open, or closed with the provider — is the choice you made when you
set the limit.

→ [How Copy Trading Executes](/docs/concepts/copy-trading-mechanics)

---

## Cancel

**Cancel subscription** from the same screen. No new trades are copied. **Existing copied positions
stay open** until you close them on the trade page — cancelling ends the copying relationship, not
your exposure to what it already opened.

---

## Following more than one provider

You can hold several subscriptions at once, in the same account or in different ones.

Spreading across several providers only diversifies if they are actually trading differently. Check
whether they take the **same markets in the same direction** — several subscriptions onto one
correlated bet is a concentrated position wearing a diversified costume. The browse page flags
providers that trade **the same basket as others**.
