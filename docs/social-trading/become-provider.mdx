# Become a Provider

Providers are listed in Social Trade and earn from followers who copy their trades. You trade your
own account; each follower's copies execute in **the follower's own account**, sized and limited by
the follower's own settings. MetaStation does not manage anyone's funds, and you never have access
to a follower's account.

---

## First decide which kind of provider you are

The signup flow asks this before anything else.

| | **Signal Provider** | **Fund Manager** |
|---|---|---|
| **Signals come from** | A webhook or a Telegram channel | Your account's trades, copied as you make them |
| **You trade** | Your own account | **Your own account** — connected exchange keys or a MetaStation Account |
| **You are paid by** | A **monthly price** you set, after an optional free trial | A **share of the profit** your followers make |
| **Followers copy into** | A MetaStation Account, or an account on your exchange | A MetaStation Account |

→ [Fund Managers](/docs/social-trading/fund-managers) covers the second kind in detail.

## Registering

{/* SCREENSHOT: provider-onboarding | Become a Provider, step 1 provider type | 1440x900 | redact: none */}
<Screenshot id="provider-onboarding" />

Open **Social Trade → Provider hub** and choose **Become a provider**
([metastation.fi/social-trade/provider/onboard](https://metastation.fi/social-trade/provider/onboard)).
Three steps:

1. **Provider Type** — Signal Provider or Fund Manager
2. **Profile** — the account your trades are read from, a picture (under 3 MB), a nickname, a
   description, and your strategy: its name, trading style, time frame and risk level
3. **Pricing** — what you charge and who can follow:

| Setting | Signal Provider | Fund Manager |
|---|---|---|
| **Monthly price (USD)** | $0 (free) to $10,000 | — |
| **Free trial (days)** | 0 to 30 | — |
| **Performance fee (%)** | — | 0, or a whole 2–99, up to your tier's limit |
| **Max followers** | Up to your tier's limit | Up to your tier's limit |
| **Minimum / Maximum allocation** | The range of what a follower may bring | Same |
| **Leverage range** | The range you tell followers you trade in | Same |
| **Telegram Group Link** | Where you post signals | — |
| **Master Trading Account** | — | The account you trade from |

Accept the terms and press **Create my provider profile**. There is no review queue: you see
*"You're listed."* and followers can find you in the browse straight away.

**The leverage range is descriptive, not enforced.** It tells a follower how leveraged your strategy
runs. It does not set the leverage on their copies — they set that themselves.

**Your price is locked in for each subscriber.** Changing your price publishes a new version: new
subscribers take it, and everyone already subscribed keeps the price they started on.

### Invite-only products

A product can be **invite only**. It stays out of the browse, and people subscribe only through an
invite link you create in the provider hub. You can label each link and withdraw it; people who
already subscribed through it keep their subscription.

## Your signals: webhook or Telegram

A Signal Provider's signals reach followers from:

- **Your webhook** — every slot has its own URL on the **Automation** page
- **A Telegram channel** you connect in **Automation**

:::warning[Real money needs a verified webhook]
Real-money accounts can copy a product only once its webhook is **signed** or **source-checked**.
On **URL only**, real-money followers are refused. Switch the webhook to **Signed requests** or the
**Source-address check** in Automation.
→ [Secure Your Webhook](/docs/guides/secure-your-webhook)
:::

## Tiers: your follower and fee limits

Every provider has a tier. It sets how many followers you can have and the highest performance fee
you can charge:

| Tier | What it takes | Followers at most | Performance fee at most |
|---|---|---|---|
| **New** | Under 30 days, or not graded yet | 50 | 10% |
| **Bronze** | Grade D or better, 30+ days, max drawdown at most 50% | 200 | 15% |
| **Silver** | Grade C or better, 90+ days, 5+ counted followers, median follower return at least 0 | 500 | 20% |
| **Gold** | Grade B or better, 180+ days, 20+ counted followers, median follower return above 0, $50k+ copied | 1,000 | 30% |

Tiers are re-checked **weekly**. A provider that qualifies higher moves up at once; one that
qualifies lower keeps its tier for a week's grace first. You see your own tier in the provider hub;
followers do not.

## The provider hub

**Social Trade → Provider hub** ([metastation.fi/social-trade/provider](https://metastation.fi/social-trade/provider)):

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Your followers and results |
| **Products** | Your products, and whether each is listed or invite only |
| **Plans** | Your monthly price and free trial, and every version you have published |
| **Signal sources** | Your webhook URLs and verification mode, and your Telegram channels |
| **Broadcasting** | Post your trades to a Telegram channel |
| **Invites** | Create and withdraw invite links |
| **Settings** | **What you receive** — the MetaStation account your payments are sent to; and **copy defaults** you suggest to new followers |

**Copy defaults** are suggestions — markets, leverage, position and margin caps, a daily loss limit,
take-profit and stop-loss, trading hours, *Only trades with a stop-loss*. New followers see them when
they subscribe and can keep or change each one.

## How you are ranked

Providers are ranked by a published **0–100 score**, never by raw return. A record needs at least
**14 days** before it is ranked. → [How Providers Are Scored](/docs/social-trading/scoring)

Your return, drawdown, trade count, follower count and what your followers made are computed from
actual trading. None of it is editable.

---

## Writing a description followers can act on

Say what you trade, on what timeframe, and how you size and exit.

Worth writing:

> "I trade BTC and ETH perpetuals on the 4-hour timeframe using support and resistance with volume
> confirmation. I risk 1–2% per trade with a fixed stop loss, and average 15–25 trades a month."

Not worth writing:

> "I trade crypto and make consistent profits."

---

## Your responsibilities

:::warning[You are not a registered financial adviser]
Publishing a strategy is not advice, and your profile should say so. Past performance does not
predict future results, and followers can and will lose money copying you.

Inflated claims, guaranteed-return promises and coordinated pump-and-dump activity are grounds for
suspension, which removes the profile from Social Trade. Anyone can report a public profile.
:::
