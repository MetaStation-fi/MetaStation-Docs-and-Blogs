# Cross-Chain Deposits

MetaStation brings **cross-chain routing and blockchain interoperability** directly into the
funding experience. You connect the Web3 wallet you already use, on whichever chain your assets
already sit, and deposit **straight into the Funding Wallet of your MetaStation Web3 Account** —
without opening a separate bridge or exchange first.

MetaStation accepts **cross-chain deposits from 56 blockchain networks**, covering major Layer 1
chains, Ethereum Layer 2 networks, EVM-compatible chains, the Bitcoin ecosystem, and leading
non-EVM ecosystems.

:::info What this page covers
This is the **funding path: any supported chain → your Funding Wallet**. The cross-chain swap
happens as part of the deposit, and the result is always **USDC margin in your MetaStation
Account**.

It is **not** a general chain-to-chain swap desk — MetaStation does not route Bitcoin to a token
on Solana for you, or move assets between two external wallets. The destination is always your
own account.

**Withdrawals are a separate, narrower flow.** They cover a specific asset set (USDT, BTC, ETH,
SOL, TRX, TON), not all 56 networks. → [Withdraw](/docs/wallet/withdraw)
:::

## Connect any Web3 wallet, on any supported chain

The step this removes is the one that usually comes first everywhere else: acquiring a specific
token on a specific chain, bridging it yourself, and holding a gas token on the destination
network before you can trade at all.

Instead:

1. Connect the wallet you already use.
2. Pick the network your assets are already on, and the asset you hold.
3. Send. It is routed cross-chain, converted, and credited as **USDC** trading margin.

So a deposit from **Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Polygon, BNB Chain, Solana, Bitcoin, TON, Tron** or
**XRP Ledger** all arrive at the same place, in the same form, ready to trade.

:::note Coverage is not uniform
Supported routes, tokens, liquidity, fees, minimums, maximums and settlement times **vary by
source network**. A network being supported does not mean every asset on it is routable. The
deposit screen shows what is actually available for the pairing you select.
:::

→ [Fund Your Account from Any Chain](/docs/guides/fund-from-any-chain)

## EVM and non-EVM blockchain support

The cross-chain infrastructure spans both **EVM-compatible and non-EVM blockchain ecosystems**, so
funding is not limited to Ethereum-style chains.

| Ecosystem | Networks include |
|---|---|
| **Ethereum and Layer 2** | Ethereum, Arbitrum One, Base, Optimism, Linea, Scroll, Taiko, Mode, Mantle, Unichain, Soneium, Manta, Metis, Boba Ethereum, Fraxtal |
| **zk rollups** | ZkSync Era, Polygon zkEVM, Cronos zkEVM, ZkLink |
| **EVM Layer 1** | BNB, opBNB, Polygon, Avalanche, Gnosis, Cronos, Sonic, Telos, Berachain, Monad, Katana, Gravity, Morph, Sei v2, Plasma, Abstract, ApeChain |
| **Bitcoin ecosystem** | Bitcoin, Merlin, Rootstock, Citrea, Goat, Bitcoin Cash |
| **Non-EVM Layer 1** | Solana, TON, Tron, XRP Ledger, Litecoin, Dogecoin, Monero, Zcash |

This is what makes MetaStation a **multi-chain crypto exchange** rather than a single-ecosystem
one: **Bitcoin bridge** deposits, **Solana cross-chain swaps**, **Tron cross-chain transfers** and
**TON cross-chain transfers** all fund the same account through the same interface as every EVM
network.

## Network availability changes

The set of available networks and assets **changes as MetaStation expands or updates its
cross-chain infrastructure**. Networks are added, and occasionally retired.

The networks, assets, routes and liquidity shown **in the MetaStation app at the time of your
transaction** are authoritative. This page reflects the integration as documented and is not a
guarantee that a particular network remains available indefinitely.

If a network you expect is missing from the picker, it is currently unavailable rather than
hidden.

## How a cross-chain deposit works

1. Open **Deposit** and make sure your **MetaStation Account slot** is selected.
2. Select your **source network** and **asset**.
3. Send from your Web3 wallet or exchange to the address shown.
4. The asset is routed cross-chain into your **Funding Wallet** — a smart-contract wallet on
   Arbitrum — and converted to USDC.
5. The USDC credits your MetaStation Account and is immediately available as trading margin.

The waiting step is confirmation on the **source** chain, which is why an Ethereum deposit takes
longer than one from Arbitrum or Base.

You never need a gas token on the destination side: gas on your trades is abstracted away.

→ [Funding and Settlement](/docs/concepts/funding-and-settlement) · [Deposit](/docs/wallet/deposit)

:::danger Match the asset and the network
The most common failed deposit is the right asset on the wrong network. Both must match what you
selected on the deposit screen. Send a small test amount first when depositing from a new wallet
or sending a large sum.
:::

:::note Connected exchange slots do not use this
A connected Binance, ByBit or KuCoin slot has **no Funding Wallet** — its balance is your balance
at that exchange, so you fund it there. Cross-chain deposits fund a MetaStation Account slot.
:::
