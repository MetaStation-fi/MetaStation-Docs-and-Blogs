# Cross-Chain Exchange

MetaStation brings **cross-chain trading and blockchain interoperability** directly into the
exchange experience. Supported digital assets can be swapped across multiple blockchain networks
without manually using separate exchanges or bridge interfaces first.

MetaStation supports **cross-chain swaps across 56 blockchain networks**, covering major Layer 1
chains, Ethereum Layer 2 networks, EVM-compatible chains, Bitcoin and Bitcoin-adjacent networks,
and leading non-EVM ecosystems.

---

## Multi-chain asset swaps

Supported assets are routed between blockchain ecosystems through one unified trading experience.
That means moving value between networks such as **Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Polygon, BNB Chain,
Solana, Bitcoin, TON, Tron, XRP Ledger** and the other supported networks without leaving
MetaStation.

In practice this removes the step that usually comes first: you do not need to acquire a specific
token on a specific chain, bridge it yourself, and hold a gas token on the destination network
before you can trade. Whatever you already hold, wherever it sits, is usable.

:::note Coverage is not uniform
Supported routes, tokens, liquidity, fees, minimums, maximums and settlement times **vary by
source and destination network**. A network being supported does not mean every asset on it is,
and the practical route for a given pair may differ from another. The deposit screen shows what is
actually available for the pairing you select.
:::

→ [Fund Your Account from Any Chain](/docs/guides/fund-from-any-chain)

## EVM and non-EVM blockchain support

MetaStation's cross-chain infrastructure spans both **EVM-compatible and non-EVM blockchain
ecosystems**, so interoperability is not limited to Ethereum-style chains.

| Ecosystem | Networks include |
|---|---|
| **Ethereum and Layer 2** | Ethereum, Arbitrum One, Base, Optimism, Linea, Scroll, Taiko, Mode, Mantle, Unichain, Soneium, Manta, Metis, Boba Ethereum, Fraxtal |
| **zk rollups** | ZkSync Era, Polygon zkEVM, Cronos zkEVM, ZkLink |
| **EVM Layer 1** | BNB, opBNB, Polygon, Avalanche, Gnosis, Cronos, Sonic, Telos, Berachain, Monad, Katana, Gravity, Morph, Sei v2, Plasma, Abstract, ApeChain |
| **Bitcoin ecosystem** | Bitcoin, Merlin, Rootstock, Citrea, Goat, Bitcoin Cash |
| **Non-EVM Layer 1** | Solana, TON, Tron, XRP Ledger, Litecoin, Dogecoin, Monero, Zcash |

This is what makes MetaStation a **multi-chain crypto exchange** rather than a single-ecosystem
one: **Bitcoin bridge** routes, **Solana cross-chain swaps**, **Tron cross-chain transfers** and
**TON cross-chain transfers** all sit behind the same interface as the EVM networks.

## Network availability changes

The set of available networks and assets **changes as MetaStation expands or updates its
cross-chain infrastructure**. Networks are added, and occasionally retired, by the underlying
routing layer.

The networks, assets, routes and liquidity shown **in the MetaStation app at the time of your
transaction** are authoritative. This page reflects the integration as documented and is not a
guarantee that a particular network will remain available indefinitely.

If a network you expect is missing from the picker, it has been retired or is temporarily
unavailable rather than hidden.

---

## How a cross-chain deposit works

1. Open **Deposit** and select your **source network** and **asset**.
2. Send from your wallet or exchange to the address shown.
3. The asset is routed cross-chain into your **Funding Wallet** and converted to USDC.
4. The USDC credits your MetaStation Account and is immediately available as trading margin.

The waiting step is confirmation on the **source** chain, which is why an Ethereum deposit takes
longer than one from Arbitrum or Base.

→ [Funding and Settlement](/docs/concepts/funding-and-settlement) · [Deposit](/docs/wallet/deposit)

:::danger Match the asset and the network
The most common failed deposit is the right asset on the wrong network. Both must match what you
selected on the deposit screen. Send a small test amount first when depositing from a new wallet
or sending a large sum.
:::
