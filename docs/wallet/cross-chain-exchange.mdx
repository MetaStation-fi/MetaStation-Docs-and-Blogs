# Cross-Chain Deposits

MetaStation brings **cross-chain routing** directly into the funding experience. You connect the
Web3 wallet you already use, on the chain your assets already sit on, and deposit **straight into
the Funding Wallet of your MetaStation Account** — without opening a separate bridge or exchange
first.

Cross-chain deposits work from **Ethereum and its Layer 2s, EVM-compatible chains, Solana and TON**.

:::info[What this page covers]
This is the **funding path: a connected wallet on a supported chain → your Funding Wallet**. The
cross-chain swap happens as part of the deposit, and the result is always **USDC in your Funding
Wallet**.

- **Sending by hand?** The Funding Wallet address accepts **USDC on Arbitrum One only**. Anything
  else sent to it by hand is lost. Use a connected wallet for every other network.
- **Bitcoin and other non-EVM chains** (Bitcoin, Litecoin, Dogecoin, XRP Ledger, Tron and similar)
  **cannot** be deposited through the Funding Wallet. Once your account is activated, BTC can be
  deposited straight into the **Trading Account** on its own network — see
  [Deposit](/docs/wallet/deposit#deposit-to-the-trading-account-other-coins).
- It is **not** a general chain-to-chain swap desk. The destination is always your own account.
:::

## Connect your Web3 wallet, on a supported chain

The step this removes is the one that usually comes first everywhere else: acquiring a specific
token on a specific chain, bridging it yourself, and holding a gas token on the destination
network before you can trade at all.

Instead:

1. On **Deposit**, pick **Funding Wallet**, then **Send from → Connect a wallet**.
2. Pick the network your assets are already on, and the coin you hold.
3. Connect your wallet: an **EVM wallet** for Ethereum-style networks, a **Solana wallet** such as
   Phantom, or a **TON wallet**.
4. Review the quote and approve. It is routed cross-chain, converted, and credited as **USDC**.

:::note[Coverage is not uniform]
Supported routes, tokens, liquidity, fees, minimums and settlement times **vary by source network**.
A network being supported does not mean every coin on it can be routed. The deposit screen shows
what is actually available for the pairing you select.
:::

→ [Fund Your Account from Any Chain](/docs/guides/fund-from-any-chain)

## Supported ecosystems

| Ecosystem | Networks include |
|---|---|
| **Ethereum and Layer 2** | Ethereum, Arbitrum One, Base, Optimism, Linea, Scroll, Taiko, Mode, Mantle, Unichain, Soneium, Manta, Metis, Boba Ethereum, Fraxtal |
| **zk rollups** | ZkSync Era, Polygon zkEVM, Cronos zkEVM, ZkLink |
| **EVM Layer 1** | BNB, opBNB, Polygon, Avalanche, Gnosis, Cronos, Sonic, Telos, Berachain, Monad, Katana, Gravity, Morph, Sei v2, Plasma, Abstract, ApeChain |
| **EVM chains in the Bitcoin ecosystem** | Merlin, Rootstock, Citrea, Goat |
| **Non-EVM** | Solana, TON |

The **network picker in the app is the live list**. Networks are added and occasionally retired as
routes change; if a network you expect is missing from the picker, it is currently unavailable
rather than hidden.

## How a cross-chain deposit works

1. Open **Deposit** and pick your **MetaStation Account** and its **Funding Wallet**.
2. Under **Send from**, pick **Connect a wallet**, then your **source network** and **coin**.
3. Connect your wallet, enter the amount, and press **Review deposit**.
4. Confirm, then approve the transaction in your wallet. The network fee is paid in your wallet's
   gas token on the source chain.
5. The deposit is routed cross-chain into your **Funding Wallet** on Arbitrum One and converted to
   USDC. On your first deposit, the one-time activation fee is taken and the account is activated.

Bridged deposits appear under **Deposits in progress** on the Deposit page until they land. One that
is delayed on the network can be sent back to your wallet with **Refund delayed deposit**.

→ [Funding and Settlement](/docs/concepts/funding-and-settlement) · [Deposit](/docs/wallet/deposit)

:::danger[Match the asset and the network]
The most common failed deposit is the right asset on the wrong network. Both must match what you
selected on the deposit screen. Send a small test amount first when depositing from a new wallet
or sending a large sum.
:::

:::note[Connected exchange slots do not use this]
A connected Binance, ByBit or KuCoin slot has **no Funding Wallet** — its balance is your balance
at that exchange. The Deposit page shows that exchange's own deposit address instead.
:::
