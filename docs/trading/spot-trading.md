# Spot Trading

Spot trading is the straightforward buying and selling of crypto assets at current market prices.

---

## Place a spot order

1. Open the **Trade** tab and select your account slot
2. Set the market type to **Spot**
3. Search for a trading pair (e.g., `BTCUSDC`, `ETHUSDT`)
4. Choose your order type
5. Enter the quantity or USD value
6. Click **Buy** or **Sell**

---

## Order types

### Market Order
Executes immediately at the best available price. Use when speed matters more than exact price.

### Limit Order
Executes only at your specified price or better. Remains open until filled or cancelled.

### Stop Market
A market order triggered when price reaches your stop level. Used to enter on breakout or limit losses.

### Stop Limit
A limit order triggered when price reaches your stop level. Gives price control, but may not fill if price moves fast.

---

## Quantity formats

All three formats are accepted:

| Format | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Token amount | `0.001` or `0.001 BTC` | Fixed token quantity |
| USD value | `$300` or `300 USD` | Spend this much USD |
| Wallet % | `2%` | Use 2% of your available balance |

---

## Take Profit and Stop Loss on spot

You can attach a Take Profit and Stop Loss to any spot position after opening it:

1. In the **Positions** panel, find your open position
2. Click **Edit TP/SL**
3. Set price levels and confirm

→ For advanced TP/SL options, see [Advanced Orders](/docs/trading/advanced-orders).
